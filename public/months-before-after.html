Months, not campaigns: prod today vs the new design

Brands buy months of content, so each campaign is now named for the month its creator content starts going live. Nothing about how campaigns unlock or run changes. What changes is a name, one date, and a small timeline card. Every frame below is the live prototype: the left one renders what production shows today, the right one the new design.

Updated Oct 2 The campaign is 8 weeks in total and first content is expected at week 6 (Julia). Before, first content was set at week 8. Every date, name and frame on this page uses the new timing.

The rule

One pure function. Everything on screen comes from the launch date. Today the portal has no dates at all on these surfaces: a card carries a number and a percent.

In words

  1. Launch date. The day the campaign was launched, in the brand's own time zone. Before launch: today for a ready campaign, the unlock date for a locked one.
  2. First content expected = launch date + 42 days. Six weeks. Julia · Oct 2
  3. The campaign is 8 weeks in total. Posts keep going live in weeks 7 and 8. Julia · Oct 2
  4. Shown as "week of" the Monday of that week, never as a single day.
  5. Month name = the month that date falls in. Exception: if it falls in the last 4 days of the month, use the next month. Julia · Oct 1
  6. Week = whole weeks since launch, plus one. Shown as "Week 4 of 8", against the 8-week total.
  7. Freeze at launch. Until then the name is recomputed every day.
# proposed home: CampaignOverview::ContentMonth (no such code exists today)
CAMPAIGN_WEEKS      = 8    # "Week 4 of 8"
FIRST_CONTENT_WEEKS = 6
CUTOFF_DAYS         = 4

def timeline(launch_date, today)
  first = launch_date + FIRST_CONTENT_WEEKS * 7   # first_content_expected_on
  late  = first.day > first.end_of_month.day - CUTOFF_DAYS
  {
    first_content_expected_on: first,
    week_of:       first.beginning_of_week(:monday),   # "week of Oct 19"
    content_month: (late ? first.next_month : first).beginning_of_month,
    week_number:   (today - launch_date).to_i / 7 + 1  # 1, 2 ... 8, then 9 ...
  }
end

#   available / saved / draft today                  -> moves every day

Where the 6 weeks come from

Week 1Match. Creators matched in 1 or 2 days, accepted in 1 or 2 more.
Weeks 2 to 3Ship. About 2 weeks for product to arrive.
Weeks 4 to 5Film. Creators have 10 days from delivery.
Week 6Review, and the first posts go live.
Weeks 7 to 8The rest of the posts go live. The campaign is 8 weeks in all.

Julia, Oct 2. This replaces the Sep 8 call's "first content at 8 weeks".

What the 4 days mean for launches

Counted on the first-content date, not the launch date. "Last 4 days" follows the real length of the month: the 28th to 31st in a 31-day month, the 27th to 30th in a 30-day month, the 25th to 28th in February (26th to 29th in a leap year).
